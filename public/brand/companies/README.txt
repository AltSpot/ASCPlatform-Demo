Company marks for the Radar board.

Drop an SVG here named <slug>.svg, then point the company's logoUrl at
it in lib/terminal/radar.ts. Slugs are openai, spacex, anthropic,
databricks.

Marks render on a warm cream plate, so a mark in the company's own
colours reads correctly whether it is black, white or coloured. Prefer
SVG; it scales with no loss. A company with no mark falls back to a
monogram on the same plate, so the board stays consistent either way.

These are third-party marks, used to identify the company being tracked.
Radar states on the page that AltSpot holds no position and is not
offering any of them.
