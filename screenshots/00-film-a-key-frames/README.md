The three the edit hangs on.

ALTSPOT-LED · SERIES A with ALTSPOT IN $600K, and its CTA is now "View
deal", not "Fund commitment". The seeded pending commitment moved to

timer beat.

Hannah Smith typed in.

**03-AFTER-transfer-youre-in** — the screen that did not exist in the
first 40 captures. It is real: gold orb, TRANSFER INITIATED, "You're in,
pending countersign," the amount, and two exits. This is the last frame
of Film A.

## Shooting note

Enter **$50,000 or more** on the amount step. The specimen subscription
agreement in the document pane states a $50,000 minimum, while Calder's
own minimum is $10,000. A smaller number puts the field and the
document in visible conflict on a screen a partner may read closely.
